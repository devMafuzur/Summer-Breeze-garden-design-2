Our Philosophy

At Summer Breeze, we believe that exceptional garden design emerges from the thoughtful integration of natural systems, architectural principles, and the unique character of each site. Our approach prioritizes sustainability, craftsmanship, and timeless aesthetic values.

Every project begins with deep listening – to the land, to our clients' aspirations, and to the inherent possibilities that exist within each space. We create landscapes that feel both inevitable and surprising, rooted in place yet elevated beyond the ordinary.

Meet Autumn

With over fifteen years of experience in landscape architecture and horticulture, Autumn Mitchell founded Summer Breeze to create gardens that transcend seasonal trends and speak to the enduring relationship between people and place.

Her design philosophy draws from extensive study of both classical garden traditions and contemporary ecological practices, resulting in landscapes that are both culturally resonant and environmentally responsive.

Autumn holds a Master of Landscape Architecture from the University of Melbourne and is a registered member of the Australian Institute of Landscape Architects.
